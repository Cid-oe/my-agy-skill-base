# Amendment RFC-0008-A1 — Executor: conform to published contracts

**Target:** RFC-0008 (Executor) · **Status:** Proposed · **Severity:** Major

## Problem

RFC-0008 Appendix A states it "was designed without the published text of
RFC-0001…0007 in hand" and assumes contracts that differ from the published
ones. Specific divergences (cross-checked against the published RFCs):

| # | RFC-0008 assumes | Published contract |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Policy: `Evaluate({action, principal, resource…}) → permit/deny/obligate` + obligations + versioned snapshots | RFC-0003 §14: `evaluateNode → ALLOW/DENY/MODIFIED/ESCALATE/DEFER`; no obligations/snapshot/principal params (now added by RFC-0003-A1) |
| 2 | Artifacts: `Create` / `Commit(refSet)` atomic manifest | RFC-0004 §9.3: `create` / `supersede`; no Commit primitive |
| 3 | Runtime State: hierarchical keys, per-key CAS, watch, leases | RFC-0005: single object per execution, domain-level CAS (now generalized by RFC-0005-A1) |
| 4 | Scheduler: can `Hold`/re-dispatch preserving priority | Actual RFC-0007 §5.7 has Ready-Queue backpressure upstream; no Order-level Hold response (now added by RFC-0007-A1 R2) |
| 5 | Skill manifests carry `compensations`, `retryability` | RFC-0002 §3 has neither (now added by RFC-0002-A1 R1) |
| 6 | Series title "Artificial General Yield" | Series is "AGY AI Operating System" |

## Reason

RFC-0008's §19 declares each divergence a compatibility note "not a license
to redesign" — this amendment is that mechanism executed. Every fix is a
name/shape conformance; the Executor's architecture (control/data planes,
Execution/Attempt split, lease+fence, PEPs, backpressure) is untouched.

## Impact

Interface names and one storage mapping change. All invariants (§5.3) and
the decision register (D-1…D-15) stand as written.

## Resolution

**R1 — Policy conformance.** PEP calls use RFC-0003-A1 R4's
`DecisionRequest` (with `principal`, `policyVersion`). Outcome mapping per
RFC-0003-A1 R3 (`permit`→ALLOW, `deny`→DENY, `obligate`→MODIFIED or
ALLOW+obligations). Fail-closed rules (§4.3) unchanged and now consistent
with RFC-0003 §13.4.

**R2 — Artifact conformance.** `Create` → `IArtifactStore.create`
(RFC-0004 §9.3, with `namespace` per RFC-0004-A1 R1). The final **result-set
manifest is an Artifact** (type `ExecutionResult`, RFC-0004 §5.2) whose
payload enumerates result/segments/logs/failure refs; `Commit(refSet)` is
expressed as `create` of that manifest — atomicity is RFC-0004's §3.2
create-atomicity, preserving invariant 4 and D-15's intent. Segment/frame
structures (§8.4) are Executor-owned artifact payload types registered in
RFC-0004's schema registry.

**R3 — Runtime State conformance.** Execution Records, Attempt Records,
command journal, and leases are **child records** of the execution's state
domain with per-key CAS, `watch`, and lease primitives as defined by
RFC-0005-A1 R2–R4 (this is the mapping RFC-0008 §19.6 requested). Stream
checkpoints (§8.4) are renamed **stream offsets** (RFC-0005-A1 R5).

**R4 — Scheduler conformance.** `Dispatch`/`Hold`/completion signals are
the operative contract jointly with RFC-0007-A1 R2/R3 (cancellation:
CANCELLING at the Scheduler on issue; terminal on the Executor's
`execution.cancelled` or budget expiry). Executor heartbeat/lease renewal
per its §11.7 stands; the Scheduler's old §11.5 heartbeat is retired.

**R5 — Naming.** Series title throughout is "AGY AI Operating System."
Future-RFC pointers are renumbered: Resource Management & Placement →
**RFC-0016**; Secrets & Identity → **RFC-0017**; Plan Teardown /
Lifecycle Governance → RFC-0021 track (escalation owner); Checkpoint &
Resume (transparent mid-skill resume) → unassigned pool; Observability →
**RFC-0015** (already published).

## Compatibility

Appendix A is downgraded to a historical record of design-time assumptions;
where it conflicts with published RFCs + this amendment, this amendment
governs. No Executor-internal behavior changes.

## Migration

Implementers: build against Appendix A **as amended by this document**.
The compatibility conformance suite (§17.9) should pin the published
contracts plus this amendment.
