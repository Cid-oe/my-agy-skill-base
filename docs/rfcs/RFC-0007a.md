# RFC-0007a: Scheduler Fairness & Anti-Starvation Policy

**Status:** Accepted (Amendment to RFC-0007)  
**Subsystem:** Scheduler  
**Author:** Lead Systems Engineer  

---

## 1. Problem Statement
RFC-0007 specifies DAG execution ordering. Under sustained multi-tenant load or large execution plans with hundreds of concurrent skill tasks, low-priority tasks must not be starved indefinitely by a continuous stream of high-priority tasks.

## 2. Policy Specification

1. **Scheduling Algorithm:**
   - **Weighted Priority-Aging Queue**:
     - Effective priority is computed as: `EffectivePriority = BasePriority + (WaitTimeInQueueMs / AgingFactorMs)`.
     - `AgingFactorMs` defaults to `5,000ms`.
     - As tasks wait in the ready queue, their effective priority monotonically increases, guaranteeing eventual dispatch.

2. **Concurrency Slots & Limits:**
   - The scheduler assigns tasks into bounded concurrency slots per tenant and per skill type to prevent any single skill from monopolizing executor worker threads.

3. **Cooperative Preemption:**
   - Tasks exceeding their leased time quantum (`Lease.expiresAt`) are flagged for cooperative yield via `ICancellationToken.isCancellationRequested`.
